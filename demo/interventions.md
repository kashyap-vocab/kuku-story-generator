# Human interventions in the demo

All made through the web app's own forms (the same calls the buttons make), logged in order.
Premise: "A delivery rider realizes every address on today's route belongs to someone who died in the same building."

## 1. Lasting instruction, at episode 5
> Open every episode with the time on Elias's delivery app, like 'The app says 3:47 PM.' Keep doing this in every episode from here on.

- Episode 5 was rewritten with it (version 1 rejected, version 2 approved).
- **Effect on later episodes:** episodes 5 to 20 all open with "The app says 3:00 AM." through "4:15 AM.", in order, without being asked again. The instruction is stored once and put into every later memory pack.

## 2. Story change that did not take, at episode 9 (kept on purpose)
> Officer Halloway dies in this part of the story. Elias is the last person to see him alive, and the building is blamed for it.

- A new plan version was created and episode 9 was rewritten.
- **Result: weak.** Halloway had not appeared in episodes 1 to 8, so the rewrite never mentioned him, and the plan still has him alive in later arcs (those lines are fixed when their arc is reached). The lesson: a kill-off only shows up where the character is already in play.

## 3. Story change, at episode 17
> Sarah Vance, the dispatcher, is dead: she died in the Oasis years ago, and the voice on Elias's phone has been the building all along.

- Sarah had appeared in episodes 3, 13 and 14.
- **Effect:** episodes 17 to 19 turn her calls into the building's voice ("transmitted directly into Elias's skull", "a layered chorus of forty different people screaming") and say she is dead.
- **Limits, honestly:** her stored status still reads "alive" (the episodes imply it without stating it), and plan lines 18 to 23 were not re-planned around her.
