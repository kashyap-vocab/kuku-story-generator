# The Last Mile: the 200-episode plan

Premise: A delivery rider realizes every address on today's route belongs to someone who died in the same building.

**Logline:** A gig-economy delivery rider discovers that his route is being manipulated by a tech firm to 'harvest' the lingering consciousness of the deceased.

**Genre:** Techno-Horror / Psychological Thriller · **Tone:** Gritty, claustrophobic, grounded

## Hidden truth (writers only)

The 'Oasis' apartment complex was a beta-test site for 'Aether-Link,' a neural-mapping startup. They discovered that high-frequency 5G signals combined with specific 'stress-inducing' architecture (the building's layout) could trap the final 60 seconds of a human's consciousness in the local WiFi mesh. The company, 'Lumina Corp,' is 'harvesting' these final thoughts to train a sentient AI. The protagonist, Elias, was hired by a shell company because his brain chemistry (due to a minor head injury from a bike accident) makes him a 'neutral' vessel—he can see the data without his mind collapsing. The 'dead' people on his route are the 40 residents who died during a structural collapse/gas leak three years ago. Lumina Corp is trying to 'complete' the data set by making Elias deliver 'triggers' (the packages) that force the Echoes to relive their final moments in high-definition.

## Style guide

- First-person perspective (Main Character).
- Present tense only.
- Short, punchy sentences. Average 10 words per sentence.
- Dialogue-heavy. Use 'said' or 'asked'—no fancy speech tags.
- No metaphors. If a room is scary, describe the smell of rot or the flickering light, don't use 'eerie' or 'sinister'.
- Use 'spoken' English: 'gonna', 'wanna', 'stuff like that'.
- Focus on sensory details: the hum of the bike, the weight of the thermal bag, the taste of cheap coffee.

## World rules

- The 'Echo' Effect: People who die in high-stress environments (like the 'Oasis' apartment complex) leave behind a digital/spiritual residue that can be 'reaped' by specific frequencies.
- The Harvest: Data isn't just stolen; it's consumed. Once a memory is harvested, the victim's 'echo' fades permanently.
- The Tether: A rider can only see the 'ghost' addresses if they are actively performing a delivery to that specific location.
- The Cost: Every time the protagonist interacts with an Echo, he loses a small piece of his own memory (starts with minor things like keys, ends with childhood memories).
- No Teleportation: The protagonist must physically travel to the locations. The mystery is solved through physical exploration of the building.

## Structure

### Act 1: The Ghost Route (episodes 1-40)
**Goal:** Elias identifies that his deliveries are repeating and begins to see the 'Echoes'. By the end, he realizes the building is a graveyard and his memories are starting to slip.

**Turning point:** Episode 40: Elias meets Marcus 'Ghost' Reed in person. Marcus acknowledges that Elias is 'special' and that his job isn't just delivery—it's 'calibration'.

Elias establishes his routine and experiences his first 'Echo' interaction. He realizes the delivery route is an impossible loop and begins to experience the first minor memory lapses.

He notices the app gives him a weirdly specific, long list of 40 individual apartment numbers to visit in one night. *Hook:* The app marks the first delivery as 'Complete' before he even leaves his bike. _(Elias Thorne)_

- **Ep 3**: Elias receives a call from Dispatch (Sarah) who tells him to 'maintain the flow' and ignore any 'glitches'. He realizes the next three addresses are all on the same floor. *Hook:* Sarah's voice glitches into a high-pitched, metallic screech for one second. _(Elias Thorne, Sarah Vance)_
- **Ep 4**: Elias delivers a small, humming black box to Apartment 405. As he sets it down, he sees a man sitting in a chair who looks like he's made of grey smoke. *Hook:* The smoke-man points at the box and says, 'It's getting louder.' _(Elias Thorne)_

He finds himself forced to finish the 40-delivery loop. *Hook:* He realizes he's been on the same floor for an hour, but the clock on his phone hasn't moved. _(Elias Thorne)_
- **Ep 6**: Elias delivers a package to Apartment 408 and feels a sharp pain in his head. He suddenly can't remember what he had for breakfast this morning. *Hook:* He looks at his hand and realizes he's holding a set of keys he doesn't recognize. _(Elias Thorne)_
- **Ep 7**: Elias meets Julian in the hallway, who is visibly shaking and clutching a framed photo. Julian tells him the building 'remembers' the people who died in the gas leak. *Hook:* Julian whispers that the 'delivery boy' is actually 'feeding' the ghosts. _(Elias Thorne, Chloe)_
- **Ep 8**: Elias enters Apartment 412 and finds a girl (Chloe) sitting on the floor. She isn't solid and speaks in a voice that sounds like it's underwater. *Hook:* She reaches out and touches his arm, making his skin go numb. _(Elias Thorne, Chloe)_
- **Ep 9**: Elias enters a lobby area and encounters Officer Halloway, who is trying to block his path to a specific door. Halloway warns him to stop the deliveries, but the building's frequency spikes, causing Halloway to collapse and die instantly. Elias stands over the body as the walls pulse with a heavy, vibrating hum. *Hook:* The app pings with a new instruction: 'Proceed to Apartment 444. Ignore the obstruction.' _(Elias Thorne, Officer Halloway)_

He hands her a small component from his bag, and she vanishes into a cloud of white static.
