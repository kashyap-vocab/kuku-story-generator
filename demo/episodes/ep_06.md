Episode 6: The Last Mile

The app says 3:00 AM.

I stand in the middle of the hallway. My feet feel heavy. The walls are peeling. The wallpaper hangs like dead skin. I try to move away from Apartment 405. My legs won't go that way. Every time I take a step left, the hallway bends. I'm walking in a circle. I look at my hands. They look weird. The skin is thin. I can see the grey of the wall through my palm. It's like I'm fading.

A sharp pain spikes in my temples. It feels like a hot needle is being driven into my brain. I try to think about my morning. I want to remember what I ate for breakfast. I know I had something. It was warm. It was... I can't see it. The memory is just a hole in my head. I look down at my left hand. The tremor is back. It's shaking so hard my arm hurts. I’m holding a ring of keys. They’re heavy. Metal. I've never seen these keys before. I don't know what they open. I don't know where they came from. They just appeared in my grip.

Suddenly, my feet move. I'm not telling them to move. My legs are just walking forward. I'm sliding down the hall like a passenger in my own body. I stop in front of Apartment 408. The door is cracked open. I walk inside.

The room smells like old grease. There's a metallic tang, too. Like wet copper. A half-eaten sandwich sits on a table. It looks bone-dry. It's not moldy. It just looks like it's been sitting there since the building was built. A small white box sits on a chair.

I feel sick. My stomach flips. The hum in the walls gets louder. It's a deep vibration. It makes my vision go fuzzy. I reach out. My fingers touch the white box.

The pain in my head doubles. It feels like a cold hand is squeezing my brain. I let out a choked noise.

I set the box down on the chair. The hum stops. The silence is heavy. It presses against my ears. I try to take a step back, but my legs are locked. I can't move a muscle. I look at the white box. A tiny light flickers on the side.

A voice comes from the box.

"Don't look behind the curtain."

It’s my voice. It sounds like me, but it's distorted. It's crackling like a bad radio. My skin goes cold. I look at the keys in my hand. I try to think about my mom. I can see her eyes. They're blue. I can see her smile. But her name is gone. I know she's my mother. I just can't find her name in my head. It's wiped clean.

I turn around. A heavy velvet curtain hangs in the corner. It's moving. It sways back and forth. Someone is breathing behind it. It's loud. It’s wet.

I look at my phone. The screen flickers.

Next Delivery: Apartment 405.

The loop is starting again. The keys in my hand start to shake. I hear a sliding sound from behind the curtain. A hand reaches out from the fabric. It’s grey. It’s long. It’s not made of skin. It's a mass of grey smoke shaped like a hand.

The smoke-hand grabs my wrist. The keys in my hand start to turn hot. They burn my skin.
