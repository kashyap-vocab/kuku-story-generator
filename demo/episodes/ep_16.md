Episode 16: The Last Mile

The app says 3:52 AM.

I push off the server rack. My legs feel like they're made of wet paper. The hum is a physical wall of pressure against my chest. It's a heavy, vibrating weight that makes my ribs ache. I need to move. I need out of this room.

I scramble toward the elevator. My fingers brush the cold steel of the door. I pull. It doesn't budge. It’s locked tight from the other side. I bang my fist against the metal, but the sound is swallowed by the roar of the servers.

I spin around and run for the stairs. 

A massive industrial gate slams down in front of me. The sound is like a car crash. It blocks the corridor, sealing me into the server room. I hit the gate with my shoulder. It doesn't give an inch. It feels like hitting a mountain of solid iron.

The hum spikes. A hot drop of blood rolls down my ear. It tastes like copper.

"Subject 41," a voice crackles. It comes from the ceiling. The intercom is distorted, making the words sound like they're being pulled through gravel. "Your session isn't over yet. Remain in the collection zone for the final sync."

I'm not a subject. I'm a person. I'm Elias. 

I try to say my name out loud, but my throat feels full of sand. My voice is a dry rasp. I look at my hands. The skin on my knuckles is so thin it's nearly transparent. I can see the grey texture of the drywall through my skin. It’s like I’m fading into the building. My body is becoming a ghost in a house of ghosts.

I try to think of my mom. I want to see her face. I want to hear her voice. I try to remember the way she smelled.

Nothing. There's just a blank space where her name should be. It’s a hole in my head. I try to remember my own last name. It's a ghost of a word. It slips away every time I try to grab it. I’m losing the pieces of me.

*Thump.*

The floor vibrates. Dust falls from the ceiling and lands on my hair.

*Thump.*

It’s coming from above. It's a heavy, rhythmic sound. Something big is moving on the floor above me. It's moving toward this room.

"Who is this?" I scream at the ceiling. My voice cracks. "Who are you?"

The intercom stays silent. The only sound is the pulsing hum and my own frantic breathing.

*Thump.*

The sound is directly over my head now. It stops. 

Then, a new sound starts. *Clack.* 

A heavy metal bolt slides back. 

A slow, grinding screech of metal on metal fills the room. The ceiling hatch begins to groan. It tilts inward, cutting into the light. 

A pair of heavy, industrial boots appears in the opening. The smell of ozone and old, metallic blood hits me first.
